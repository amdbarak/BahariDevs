# 04 — Content, SEO, and Analytics

## 1. Voice

- confident, precise, helpful;
- outcome-led rather than jargon-led;
- professional enough for government and enterprise buyers;
- understandable to SMEs and individual customers;
- no exaggerated superlatives without evidence;
- short paragraphs and descriptive headings;
- English at launch with translation-ready content architecture.

## 2. Information architecture

Primary navigation:
- Home
- Services
- Solutions
- Industries
- Work
- Insights
- About
- Contact
- Request Service

Utility:
- Track Request
- Admin/Staff Sign In

## 3. Page requirements

### Home
- differentiated hero and primary CTA;
- selected services;
- priority industries;
- why BahariDevs;
- operating process;
- approved evidence/case studies;
- trust content;
- FAQs;
- conversion CTA.

### About
- company story;
- mission, vision, values;
- service philosophy;
- founder message only when supplied;
- legal/company details only when verified.

### Service detail
- problem framing;
- ideal customer/use cases;
- deliverables;
- benefits;
- process;
- delivery modes;
- pricing mode;
- FAQs;
- related work/industries;
- request CTA.

### Solutions and industries
Avoid duplicate thin pages. Each page needs distinct audience problems, service combinations, operating considerations, and CTA.

### Portfolio
Case-study fields:
- title;
- confidentiality mode;
- industry;
- client name only when approved;
- problem;
- constraints;
- approach/process;
- solution;
- technologies;
- timeline;
- outcome with approved evidence;
- media;
- related services;
- SEO.

### Blog
- drafts and scheduled publishing;
- categories and tags;
- authors;
- related content;
- readable article layout;
- sanitized rich content;
- preview;
- SEO/social fields.

## 4. Technical SEO

Required:
- unique title and meta description;
- canonical URL;
- Open Graph and social metadata;
- XML sitemap;
- robots rules;
- semantic headings;
- breadcrumb navigation and structured data;
- Organization/LocalBusiness schema after verified details;
- Service schema where appropriate;
- Article schema;
- breadcrumb schema;
- stable slugs and redirect records;
- noindex for drafts, previews, admin, tracking, internal search, and sensitive pages;
- custom 404/410/500;
- image dimensions and alt text;
- pagination/canonical handling;
- clean server-rendered content.

Do not create doorway pages for every location. Local SEO pages require distinct useful content.

## 5. Analytics and consent

No analytics provider is assumed.

Architecture:
- first-party event abstraction;
- optional privacy-respecting provider added only after approval;
- no marketing cookies before consent;
- essential cookies documented;
- avoid collecting request form content in analytics;
- IP anonymization/configuration where supported;
- respect Do Not Track/global privacy signals where practical;
- retention and access documented.

Suggested events:
- service CTA;
- request start;
- request step completion;
- request submitted;
- tracking link opened;
- contact submitted;
- quote-request CTA;
- case study viewed.

## 6. Content readiness gates

The following cannot be published as real content until supplied and approved:
- testimonials;
- client logos;
- quantitative results;
- completed project narratives;
- team/founder profiles;
- certifications;
- legal registration details;
- guarantees;
- photography represented as company-owned work.

## 7. Content model

CMS records should support:
- status: draft/scheduled/published/archived;
- publish timestamps;
- author/editor;
- slug and redirects;
- SEO title/description;
- canonical override only for admin;
- social image;
- revision metadata;
- preview token;
- publication audit.

# 00 — Master Project Specification

## 1. Product

Build a complete, production-oriented digital platform for **BahariDevs Limited**, a premium, trustworthy, innovative technology-services company headquartered in Urban West, Zanzibar and serving Zanzibar, mainland Tanzania, and suitable international remote clients.

The platform is a modular Laravel monolith with three integrated experiences:

1. **Public website and conversion system**
2. **Service-request and operations system**
3. **Administration and content-management system**

The primary first-release business outcome is to **generate qualified leads**, while creating a reliable foundation for operational service management.

## 2. Product principles

- Trust before spectacle
- Clear outcomes before technical jargon
- Original design rather than template imitation
- Fast, accessible, mobile-first interactions
- Secure-by-default guest workflows
- Manual operational control before risky automation
- Traceable state changes and communications
- Shared-hosting compatibility
- Progressive enhancement and graceful failure
- No false production-readiness claims

## 3. Audiences in priority order

1. Government institutions
2. Large companies
3. Clinics and hospitals
4. Hotels and tourism businesses
5. Small and medium businesses
6. Schools
7. Individual customers
8. Retail businesses
9. International clients suitable for remote digital services

## 4. Differentiators to communicate

- Reliability
- Innovation
- Speed
- Expertise
- Responsive support
- Affordable, proportionate solutions

These are positioning themes, not unsupported guarantees. Final claims require approved evidence.

## 5. v1 scope

### Public website
- Home
- About
- Services and individual service detail pages
- Solutions
- Industries
- Portfolio/case studies
- Blog
- Contact
- Request-service entry points
- Privacy, terms, cookie information, accessibility statement, and acceptable-use pages
- Search-friendly, shareable, measurable content structure

### Operations
- Guest service-request submission
- Secure guest tracking
- Multiple service items per request
- Private attachments
- Priorities
- item-level workflow
- lead and supporting technician assignment
- technician specialties
- scheduling and conflict prevention
- service zones and travel fees
- public and internal messaging
- completion evidence and admin approval
- email and WhatsApp notification adapters
- quotations
- invoices
- manual recording of externally completed/offline payments solely to support receipt issuance
- no online payment collection

### Administration
- Requests and service items
- assignments and schedules
- users and roles
- technicians and specialties
- quotes, invoices, manual payment records, receipts
- blog and portfolio
- SEO fields
- analytics
- audit logs
- provider/settings configuration
- privacy requests and retention jobs

## 6. v1 exclusions

- Public client accounts or self-registration
- Online payment gateways
- Kiswahili interface/content
- Technician GPS/live location
- inventory management
- accounting ledger or full ERP
- native mobile applications
- automated technician assignment
- live-chat widget
- unmoderated inbound WhatsApp/email automation
- AI-generated customer-facing advice or estimates

## 7. Delivery model

Development occurs through the numbered task files. Each task is independently reviewable and ends with a stop point. No task begins without owner approval.

## 8. Production meaning

“Production-ready” means all applicable acceptance criteria pass, real provider credentials are configured outside source control, hosting and cron/queue behavior are verified, approved content is loaded, backups and restore are tested, security/privacy review is complete, and unresolved launch-blocking risks are closed.

Code generation alone is not production certification.
